NEXUS HACKATHON 2026

Nexus Hackathon is a hackathon organized by GLS University.

The hackathon focuses on developing innovative technology-based solutions to real-world problems.

The final round of the Nexus Hackathon is scheduled between 14th September and 16th September.

Participating teams are expected to have a working Proof of Concept (POC) ready for the final evaluation.

During the final evaluation, teams may be asked about their problem understanding, research, requirements, significance, proposed solution, technical feasibility, and implementation.

Teams should be prepared to explain their project clearly and demonstrate their working solution.

The final evaluation may include technical questions from judges regarding the technologies, architecture, implementation, and feasibility of the proposed solution.

Teams are also expected to remain flexible and adapt their solution based on feedback received from industry experts or evaluators.

The Nexus Hackathon encourages students to build practical and innovative solutions using modern technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, software development, databases, and other relevant technologies.

The hackathon provides an opportunity for students to demonstrate their technical knowledge, problem-solving skills, research, and ability to develop real-world applications.
